Clean take.

Silence is what a room sounds like when someone is listening.

What happened. Nobody was singing. After nine hours on one song, your ears kept playing it — a voice at the mic, a chord at the piano, a kit with no drummer. Every time you clicked to find it, you listened harder, and the song got louder. You were the one composing it.

Why. Engineers know this: listen to silence long enough and the ear fills it with what it expects. The phantoms were never on the recording. The only way to a clean take was to stop reaching for them.
